The Story

From tinkering after school to shipping software for clients worldwide.

I started coding at age 14 because I wanted to build tools that solved my own day-to-day problems. What began as modifying game scripts and building simple calculators evolved into deep obsession with clean software design, system architecture, and performance.

Over the last three years, as a "teenlancer" and self-driven developer, I turned that obsession into real-world freelance projects and competitive hackathon projects. I don't just write code; I care about why the code exists: helping a local business generate bookings, giving an early-stage startup an MVP they can pitch to investors, or making an interface accessible to people with disabilities.

When I’m not coding, you’ll find me contributing to open-source toolkits, experimenting with new browser APIs, running 5k trail routes, or mentoring peers who want to learn programming.
